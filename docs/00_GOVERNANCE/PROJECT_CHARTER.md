# OrganHeal Project Charter

Version: 1.0

Status: Active

Owner: Hussam

---

# Mission

Build the world's most trusted Personal Health Intelligence Platform.

---

# Vision

Transform fragmented medical information into trusted health intelligence that helps people make better long-term health decisions.

---

# Core Product Goal

Every interaction inside OrganHeal should help users understand:

- Where they are.
- What matters most.
- What to do next.
- Why it matters.

---

# Engineering Goal

Create an architecture that can evolve for years without requiring major rewrites.

---

# Clinical Goal

Improve health understanding without replacing licensed medical professionals.

---

# Business Goal

Build a sustainable subscription platform that delivers continuous health value instead of one-time report analysis.

---

# Success Definition

OrganHeal succeeds when:

- Users understand their health better.
- Doctors receive clearer summaries.
- The platform remains fast.
- The platform remains trustworthy.
- The platform scales efficiently.
