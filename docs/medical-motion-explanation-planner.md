# Voice quality and adaptive explanation planning

R2.5C/R2.6 extend the existing internal-review Medical Motion foundation. There
is no website endpoint, delivery activation, new organ geometry or replacement
render engine. All plans remain non-executable and patient-facing false.

## Voice and supporting audio

The six existing AR/EN CALM, DEEP and WARM_GUIDE definitions retain their identity.
The R2.5B Arabic cedar evidence remains accepted; no other voice or future variant
becomes executable merely because an audition was generated. Audition records
pin generated bytes, duration, latency and content checks. Subjective listening
scores and cost remain null when unavailable. FINAL_SIGNATURE stays false.
The bounded pronunciation registry is editorial audition material, not a medical
dictionary or a guarantee of provider pronunciation. Existing provider controls
remain adapter-local. No cloning, person imitation or medical script substitution.

The five supporting audio categories remain policy-only: no licensed, reviewed
assets have been admitted. Clinical and patient education both default OFF.
Client booleans or claimed license clearance cannot activate assets. Future
admission must use trusted asset/license authority and explicit recipe opt-in.
The fixed mix policy caps support at -24 dB and -36 dB under narration, with
300/500 ms fades and 150/800 ms attack/release targets. These are configuration
targets, not evidence of an executed loudness/ducking acceptance. Arbitrary dB
values/filter strings, alarms, monitor imitation and pathological heartbeat cues
are unavailable. Narration is primary; no support audio is added to auditions.

## Evidence and planning authority

`readMedicalExplanationContext` accepts only a trusted server-owned reader that
must authorize the owner/reference, classify source records and supply immutable
revision evidence. It snapshots bounded content and issues a process-local opaque
context; copied JSON cannot plan as authorized context. The reader is an adapter
boundary, not a public API: wiring an authenticated repository is required before
personal-context product use. Demo readers contain synthetic educational records.
Arbitrary request text and supplied evidence classifications cannot become facts.

USER_STATED remains unconfirmed. LAB/REPORT_VERIFIED describe source authority,
not disease certainty. SYSTEM_DERIVED and RISK_ESTIMATE remain qualified.
CONFIRMED_DIAGNOSIS attributes an existing authorized record; the planner does
not diagnose. Symptom plans require the existing clinical safety gate before any
execution. No urgency/triage logic is replaced by planning.

The planner supports seven structured request modes. Request interpretation/LLM
assistance can follow later; this milestone consumes bounded, explicit inputs.
Personal and creator lanes are separate. Creator planning permits general
education only and rejects non-educational context. Entitlements are not granted.

## Transparent timing

QUICK 20–45s; STANDARD 45–90s; DETAILED 60–150s; COMPREHENSIVE 120–240s.
Target count, evidence groups, cross-organ dependencies, terminology, safety,
comparison and next-action requirements determine a bounded complexity score.
Requested depth supplies the starting duration class; comprehension requirements
may promote the class. There is no silent truncation of required explanation.

Narration uses editorial planning rates of 115 Arabic and 130 English words/minute,
semantic pauses and transition pauses. These are estimates, not invented word
timestamps. A supplied server-side measured duration overrides the narration
estimate while structural comprehension and class minima remain intact. Measurement
is planning metadata, not trusted voice/render authority: execution must still use
the existing measured-audio capability, ownership and Medical AV Sync contracts.

Explanations above 240s split deterministically into at most eight chapters. All
chapter budgets sum to the total, target groups are contiguous, and single-target
long explanations carry continuation sections. Each chapter gets its own scene
allocation. This does not bypass the current Voice Runtime V1 60s intake limit:
long-form executable narration needs bounded section orchestration in a later
milestone, never a relaxed audio limit or motion time stretch.

## Visuals and caching

Only opaque compiled cinematic capabilities supply master availability. The
existing hero can support generic heart education, not addressable structure
highlighting. Unsupported kidney/structure targets use narration-only segments;
no missing organ, plaque, blockage, enlargement or pathological rhythm is invented.
ESTABLISH/ORIENT/APPROACH/FOCUS/EXPLAIN/REORIENT are allocated by fixed weights,
not equally. FOCUS is a teaching intent, never unsupported anatomical labels.
Motion speed remains 1; source boundaries, master hashes, camera caps and native
cycles are unchanged. Reuse is preferred and no Blender call is made by planning.

INSTANT_COMPOSE/CACHED_SCENE_COMPOSE are currently planned; SEMI_CUSTOM_RENDER
and CUSTOM_RENDER are reserved classifications and cannot execute here. Cache
availability does not prove a storage hit, so expectedCacheHit remains null.
Cost remains null. Safe future UX exposes depth/audience, not provider/render
internals. Planner identities include versions, context revision and source pins.
